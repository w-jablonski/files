# Polish+English Keyboard Character Layouts 
# Polsko-angielskie układy znaków na klawiaturze

### Layout *wmkb*

The layout is unique in that it doesn't use the lowest pinkie keys — I have layer activators or modifiers there and I recommend this.<br>
For one, this allows Ctrl+XCV without breaking fingers. 
See my whole setup at a separete [repo](https://github.com/w-jablonski/double_podium). 
See the layout's [statistics](https://cyanophage.github.io/playground.html?layout=qdczv%3Dguyx%3Bnsrtlhaeio%27kwmb*fp%2C.j%5C%2F-&mode=ergo&lan=polish&thumb=l) at Cyanophage's excellent Layout Playground.

Factors influencing the layout:
* I'm using a straight grid keboard (not ergonomic or columnar stagger) so upper pinkies are pretty hard to reach
* My right hand sits at an angle (while the left is more straight). This makes the HE lateral stretch and H>A slide comfortable.<br>Q requires quite a stretch, though

Design choices:
* For simplicity vowels are together and A, E, I, O make the home row. The frequent IE must be a comfortable roll with strong fingers
* Frequent bigrams RZ, SZ, CZ, DZ are inward rolls and N, S, R, T are chosen for the home row. Put together, this places Z in the inner column and then T with it, as TZ is rare
* Hands are kept "up". Dropping to bottom row, especially with index and middle fingers, feels slow. This runs against the "curl" principle as I understand it and touches C, Z, L, H
* SZCZ needs to be comfy. This is subtle but for one, S and C are on different columns
* X, C, V cannot be adjacent to where they originally were. I have Ctrl+XCV mapped to their original positons and hitting e.g. Ctrl+C to accomplish Ctrl+V would be weird

### Recommendation

Another person is working on a Polish+English layout and their results seem to be much better than mine so far. Their layout has been published only (?) in 
this Reddit [thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/KeyboardLayouts/comments/1leon5u/keyboard_layout_advice_enpl/). I allow myself to quote it (at the time of writing, Apr 2026):
```
p g l m f   q o u y d
n s r t j   x a e i h
b c w z v   ? _ , . k
```
It exhibits similarities to my own *wmkb* layout. Placing O above A looks interesting. I indeed tried O above E and didn't quite like it:<br>
the finger felt uncertain and I would often mistype these — but with the vowel pair now at the index finger the uncertainty might be<br>
all gone.
